Longtittle Snodgrass

A small, sharp-eyed fairy of the deep wood, quiet on the trail and quick with a blade — trailing a stack of letters that call him an arsonist.

Longtittle Snodgrass is a woodgrue hunter — one of the small, impish demi-fey who dwell in Dolmenwood’s deepest thickets, and a woodwise tracker besides. Barely three-and-a-half feet tall but three-score years old, he moves unseen through the undergrowth, reads a trail at a glance, and fights close with dagger and shortsword. Neutral in his loyalties, and dogged by a sheaf of accusing letters he would rather not discuss.
