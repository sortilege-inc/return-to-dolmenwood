The Rules of Dolmenwood

Dolmenwood is played on the bones of Old-School Essentials — quick to learn, deadly if taken lightly. The complete rules live in the official online reference; the sections below map what you’ll find there.

❦ Open the Dolmenwood Rules Reference ↗

Starting Play

Character statistics, ability scores, alignment, advancement, and languages.

Kindreds

The playable folk of the wood: Breggle (goat-folk), Elf, Grimalkin (fairy cats), Human, Mossling, and Woodgrue.

Classes

Bard, Cleric, Enchanter, Fighter, Friar, Hunter, Knight, Magician, and Thief.

Magic

Arcane magic, Fairy magic, Holy magic, and Mossling knacks — including the enchanter’s runes and sigils.

Equipment, Services & Animals

Adventuring gear, arms and armour, mounts and hounds, lodging, pipeleaf, fungi and herbs, retainers, and specialist services.

Adventuring

Time and movement, encumbrance, hazards, travel and camping, settlements, dungeons, encounters, and combat.

New to the wood? Start with Starting Play and Kindreds, then read the Adventuring core rules. That’s enough to walk in. Everything else you can look up at the table.

This Table’s House Rules

Every table rules the wood a little differently. Ours are kept on the campaign’s own page — read them before your first expedition, because they change how travel, downtime, and death play out.

Joining an Expedition

This is a West Marches game: no fixed session time, no fixed party. Players muster a group, name a destination, and set out. Scheduling, rosters, and sign-ups all live on the community page.
